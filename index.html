Geospatial data scientist · San Francisco

Jackson Harris

I use satellite data to find out where forests are being cleared, what's being grown or mined there, and who's buying it. Lately I've also been modeling carbon removal from enhanced rock weathering. I've spent about six years on this, working with researchers, campaigners, governments and companies.

Coffee on recently cleared forest in Central America

Overlaid the Forest Data Partnership's new coffee maps on Hansen forest loss for Honduras, Guatemala, Nicaragua and El Salvador. Found a few concentrated hotspots (Olancho, Alta Verapaz, Jinotega) and delivered a ranked list of field-check targets, including clearing after the EUDR cut-off.

2025–26 · Terradot

River chemistry and downstream carbon loss from enhanced weathering

Built Python and PostGIS pipelines that combine field measurements, hydrochemistry and carbonate-system modeling for enhanced rock weathering projects in Brazil. The work fed into site selection, MRV and project design documents. I also helped assemble and curate the field data used in From Weather to Enhanced Weathering, a Terradot and Microsoft paper at the NeurIPS 2026 climate workshop.

2024 · Mighty Earth

From Forests to EVs: deforestation in the nickel supply chain

Mapped forest loss around Indonesian nickel mines and smelters and traced it to battery and EV supply chains. The white paper helped push companies toward commitments on transparency and ecosystem rehabilitation, and the Kabaena Island findings were covered by the Associated Press.

2020–25 · Mighty Earth

Global deforestation rapid response

Ran a monitoring program that linked new clearing to palm oil, soy, beef and cocoa suppliers. I built the pipelines, organized ground-truthing trips, and turned the findings into evidence for corporate engagement and press. That included interactive tools for the governments of Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire. Example reports: cocoa, cattle, palm oil. The cattle work fed into a New York Times investigation into chemical clearing of the Brazilian Amazon.

Mighty Earth

Cocoa Accountability Map

An interactive Earth Engine map of deforestation in cocoa regions of Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire and Cameroon. It overlays alerts on supply-chain data covering more than 2,000 cocoa cooperatives, so companies, regulators and the public can see where clearing sits near their suppliers.

2025 · Mighty Earth

Cameroon on the brink: cocoa's new deforestation frontier

I wrote this report on Cameroon, where a cocoa boom is driving new clearing in districts like Nkondjock, Yabassi and Njombe. It traces how cocoa from recently cleared land reaches exporters through informal intermediaries. Editorial support came from colleagues at Mighty Earth and investigation support from CODED. I also helped raise the $100,000 grant that funded the monitoring.

Machine learning on OMI satellite data

About

I studied geography and computer science at Dartmouth. Since then I've worked between the data and the people who act on it: the analyses I build go to companies deciding where to source from, governments designing traceability systems, and reporters checking a story.

I'm most interested in forest restoration, wildfire resilience, nature-based climate solutions and coastal ecosystems, especially in Latin America. Lately I've been getting into drone-based monitoring.

Tools: Python, SQL/PostGIS, R, Google Earth Engine, QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Felt, JavaScript/React.
